# Newsletter plan — ODDletters

The best asset ODD owns: **1,514 active subscribers at a 60.9% median open rate**,
roughly double the industry norm. Also the most neglected: **silent for 80 days**,
shrinking, growing at 4.5 signups a month, and 92% unattributed.

Evidence: [`../intel/self/newsletter-audit.md`](../intel/self/newsletter-audit.md).

---

## 1. Fix these before sending anything

Four jobs, none of which need a decision from anyone.

| # | Job | Why | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Add UTMs to every outbound link** — site, Instagram bio, zaap.bio, LinkedIn, Discord | 92% of subscribers are attributed "direct". Relaunch without this and we learn nothing, again. | 1 hour |
| 2 | **Add the five segment tags** (`participant`, `member`, `delegate`, `partner`, `curious`) and backfill from ticket, membership and ODDference records | Every send currently goes to all 1,514 identically. | 1 afternoon |
| 3 | **Establish the consent basis for the August 2025 import** — 1,589 people, 84% of the list | They bought a ticket. Whether they agreed to a newsletter is a different question, and it is a legal one. | Ronny |
| 4 | **Find the duplicate-send bug** | On 2026-03-27 the same email went to two near-identical segments an hour apart. Still in the workflow. | 30 min |

Also worth an hour: the **December 2025 cohort retained 8 of 44 signups (18%)** where
every other month retains 90–100%. Something acquired 36 people who left immediately.
Find out what, so no acquisition push repeats it.

## 2. The re-open send

**The highest-stakes email in the plan.** 80 days of silence, a list that has never
been marketed to, and a 60% open rate to protect.

**It must not be a rebrand announcement.** "We changed our name" is about ODD. The
click data says the audience acts on usefulness and on being asked to take part —
"Everything You Need to Know" (9.46%) and "New Ways to Get Involved" (8.41%) — and
opens for news and emotion. A name change is neither.

**Shape:**

1. **Acknowledge the silence in one line.** Do not over-explain. The
   vulnerability-plus-triumph register is the one that has demonstrably worked for
   ODD — the 533-reaction LinkedIn post, the 113-like Instagram post, and the
   "Thank You" email that clicked 8.56%.
2. **What happened since June.** Short. Specific. Named people.
3. **One primary ask, segmented:**
   - `delegate` → ODDference 2027 early access
   - `member` → renewal, bring someone
   - `participant` → the 2027 gate, tickets
   - `partner` → the Impact Report and a conversation
   - `curious` → follow `@oddfest.co`, come to an ODDspace event
4. **One line on the website**, at the bottom, as information rather than news.

**Do not** send the same email to all five. That is the whole point of job 2.

## 3. Cadence

**Monthly.** Not weekly.

Soaked by Slush ran monthly with a full editorial team. ODD has no content hire and
Ronny's attention is the scarcest input in the system. A cadence that breaks is worse
than a slower one that holds — and ODDletters has already demonstrated it breaks:
20 sends, then 80 days of nothing.

Twelve good emails a year to 1,514 people who open 60% of them is a serious channel.
Fifty-two mediocre ones is a way of stopping in March.

## 4. Structure of a normal issue

Derived from what actually clicks, not from a template.

| Section | Why |
|---|---|
| **One person** | Person-led content wins 2.5–3× on every channel measured. |
| **What happened** | Proof, short, with photos that already exist on Flickr. |
| **What you can do** | The clicking section. Utility and participation are what move. |
| **One ask** | Segment-specific. |

**Not** a lineup drop. Four of twenty sends were lineup announcements and they are
the four worst-clicking emails ODD has ever sent (2.00%, 2.07%, 3.01%, 5.00%).

## 5. Growth

At 4.5 signups/month against 13.8% decay across 20 sends, the list dies of attrition
regardless of open rate. Three mechanics, in order of leverage:

| Mechanic | Source | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **Capture in the room** | The Room Loop — [`04-room-loop.md`](04-room-loop.md) | The big one. 5,000+ people have been in an ODD room; 1,514 are reachable. Every ODDspace event is a capture opportunity currently being wasted. |
| **"Comment ODD" → DM the link** | Buildspace, 14× on comments | Converts Instagram's 4,409 into subscribers. Instagram has produced **4 subscribers ever**; almost any number beats it. |
| **Publish the archive on the web** | Soaked | 15 web views/issue means every ODD newsletter is invisible to search. Indexable pages compound; email does not. |

## 6. What we track

Four numbers, monthly. Nothing else.

| Metric | Baseline, 2026-09-10 | Direction |
|---|---|---|
| Active subscribers | **1,514** | Must stop falling. Growing is the goal. |
| Signups/month | **4.5** | Target 40+, which is a 10× on a very low base. |
| Median open rate | **60.9%** | Protect. If this drops below 50% something is wrong. |
| Attribution: % `direct` | **92.1%** | Should fall below 50% within two months of job 1. |

## 7. The identity question

The publication sits on `oddfest.beehiiv.com`, named *ODDletters*, under the
organisation *"Ronny's Hiiv"*. Blocked as **B15** and gated on **D1**.

**Recommendation: do not migrate this quarter.** The list is 1,514 and acquiring 4.5
people a month — it cannot absorb subscriber loss from a re-domain, and there is no
evidence any subscriber cares about the URL. Rename the *organisation* label if it is
embarrassing; leave the domain until the list is growing.
