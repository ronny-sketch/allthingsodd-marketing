# Channel playbooks

One page per channel: what it is for, who it talks to, what goes on it, what does
not, how often, who owns it, and how we know it is working.

**The rule that governs all of them:**

> **Instagram carries the feeling. Email carries the instruction. LinkedIn carries
> the credibility. Discord carries the belonging.**

Sending the wrong one to the wrong place is the most common current error and it is
free to fix.

---

## Instagram — `@oddfest.co` (4,409) + `@oddspace.co` (510)

**ODD's largest owned audience.** Treat it accordingly.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Audience** | Participant, Curious |
| **Job** | Make people want to be in the room. Nothing else. |
| **Owner** | Needs one named person. Currently ambiguous. |
| **Cadence** | 3–4 posts/week in season, 1–2 out of season. Stories daily when something is happening. |

**What goes on it**

1. **People, named.** One person, one specific thing they made or did. This is the
   highest-performing format across every benchmark and both of ODD's own channels.
2. **Moments.** Proof something real happened. Photos, 15-second clips, one line.
3. **Gratitude.** ODD's best-performing post is a thank-you (121 likes).
4. **Reposts of other people's posts about ODD.** `@smilefestival.de` got 135 and
   `@aes.kuva` 114 — both beat ODD's own median of ~43. This content is free and
   better than what ODD makes itself.

**What does not go on it**

- Feedback forms (21 likes), partner thank-you carousels (26), admin of any kind.
  Put these in Stories or in email.
- Lineup-only posts with no person attached.
- Anything that would read the same coming from any other festival.

**The one mechanic to add:** the Buildspace **"comment X → DM the link"** loop.
Twelve posts using it averaged 2,791 comments against 196 without — a **14×**
multiplier — and it works at 4,000 followers as well as 200,000 because it converts
in the DMs rather than through the single bio link. One post: *"Comment ODD and we'll
send you the open call."*

**Fix first:** the live site links to `@oddfest.fi`, which does not exist. Also,
`zaap.bio/oddfest` is the real front door for 4,409 people and is neither branded nor
measured.

**Working =** comments and DMs rising; newsletter signups tagged `instagram` rising
from a baseline of **4 ever**.

## LinkedIn — people first, page second

| | |
|---|---|
| **Audience** | Delegate, Partner |
| **Job** | Credibility and revenue. |
| **Owner** | Ronny for the personal account; one person for the page. |
| **Cadence** | Ronny 2×/week. Page 1×/week, mostly amplifying people. |

**The doctrine.** Ronny's post about ODDfest 2025 drew **533 reactions and 70
comments**. The 3,341-follower company page has produced nothing close. The same
2.5–3× person-over-page multiplier shows up on Instagram and in the Buildspace
teardown. So:

> **LinkedIn is a squad, not a page.** Eleven core team members, plus speakers,
> artists and members who already post voluntarily. The page exists to be tagged and
> to hold the canonical version.

**What works, from ODD's own record:** the 533-reaction post was logged at the time
as the *"vulnerability + triumph formula"* — "we did something brave, it did not end
perfectly, we took a loss, we learned." Ronny's Instagram post in the same register
drew 113 likes on 3,626 followers (3.1%, ~3× the brand rate).

That is the voice that works, and it is the one nobody else can copy.

**What goes on it**

1. **First-person accounts of real decisions**, including the ones that went badly.
2. **Named people** — a speaker, a member, a partner, with the specific thing they do.
3. **Evidence** — the Impact Report, real numbers, what a partnership produced.
4. **Direct asks.** LinkedIn is where Delegates and Partners are. Ask them.

**The under-used asset:** the ODDference LinkedIn kit and 3-variant Canva templates
already exist in `wiki/projects/social-kits/`. They were built for 2026 and the weak
link was distribution, not creation. Send them again.

**Working =** ODDference pre-buys; inbound partner conversations; replies, not
reactions.

## Newsletter — ODDletters (1,514 active, 60.9% open)

The best asset ODD owns. Full plan in
[`03-newsletter-plan.md`](03-newsletter-plan.md).

| | |
|---|---|
| **Audience** | All five, **segmented** |
| **Job** | The destination. Where the instruction lives and where money is asked for. |
| **Cadence** | **Monthly.** Soaked by Slush ran monthly with a full editorial team. |

**What goes in it:** utility and the next step. The click data is unambiguous —
"Everything You Need to Know" 9.46%, "New Ways to Get Involved" 8.41%; lineup drops
2.0–3.0%. **Stop leading with programming.**

**Working =** list growing rather than shrinking (it has fallen 1,693 → 1,460 across
20 sends); click rate holding above 6%; attribution no longer 92% "direct".

## The website — allthingsodd.co

| | |
|---|---|
| **Audience** | Partner and Delegate doing due diligence; Curious arriving from a link |
| **Job** | Make ODD look real, and convert to the list. |

**Two structural gaps:**

1. **No analytics at all.** GA4 and Search Console both return zero records
   (blocker B16). Everything else here is unmeasurable until this is fixed.
2. **No searchable body of ODD writing exists on the open web.** The newsletter
   averages **15 web views** per issue. Publish it as indexable pages — this is what
   Soaked understood when it called itself *media* and used the newsletter as the
   subscription mechanism rather than the product.
