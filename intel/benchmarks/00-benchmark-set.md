# The benchmark set — who we study, and why

Selection rule: each one has to answer a question ODD actually faces. "Successful
creative platform" is not a reason. Where an obvious candidate was rejected, the
reason is recorded, because rejections are where selection bias hides.

## Tier 1 — structural twins

These share ODD's actual shape: a non-profit or independent body running a festival,
a professional programme, and a physical space, under one name.

| # | Who | Where | Answers |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Arty Farty** — Nuits Sonores, European Lab, Le Sucre, H7 | Lyon | The closest analogue that exists. Non-profit association, 83% self-financed, festival + forum + venues. Same legal shape as New Nordic Way rf, same product spread as ODDfest + ODDference + ODDspace, twenty years ahead. |
| 2 | **Horst Arts & Music** | Vilvoorde, BE | Explicitly three pillars — Festival, Club, Atelier — on a city-granted site (Asiat Park). ODDspace is a 50/50 revenue split with YIT on Sturenportti 3. Same bet: a festival brand that took custody of a building. |
| 3 | **Sónar / Sónar+D** | Barcelona | The canonical festival-plus-conference pair, and the canonical answer to the brand-architecture question ODD is asking right now. |
| 4 | **Slush** | Helsinki | Same city, same ecosystem, same funders, same volunteer engine — and it has already run and *retired* the exact newsletter-and-community sub-brands ODD is about to build. |

## Tier 2 — mechanics we want to steal

Studied for one specific thing each, not in the round.

| # | Who | Studied for |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | **Buildspace** | The audience-acquisition machine. Already torn down — see [buildspace.md](buildspace.md). |
| 6 | **OFFF** | Annual identity refresh against a fixed wordmark — the question of how much ODD's visual system should change per edition. |
| 7 | **Dekmantel** | Festival → label → store → radio. What a culture brand's owned-media stack looks like when it works. |
| 8 | **Le Guess Who?** | Curation-as-brand: guest curators as the marketing mechanic. Cheap, and ODD has the network for it. |
| 9 | **MUTEK** | Multi-city editions under one name — the only benchmark that has solved the "ODDfest in another country" problem. |
| 10 | **It's Nice That** | The creative-media publishing model ODD's newsletter and podcast would sit next to. Also a competitor for the same attention. |

## Tier 3 — local comparators

Not aspirational. These are who ODD is actually compared to in Helsinki, by the same
funders and the same press.

| # | Who | Note |
|---|---|---|
| 11 | **Flow Festival** | The Helsinki brand-and-partnership benchmark. Thomas McGinley's line — "imagine if Slush and Flow Festival had a love child" — is on record as an organic description of ODDfest. That framing is either an asset or a trap and we should know which. |
| 12 | **Roskilde** | The non-profit festival that funds a foundation. Where ODDfest's own non-profit logic points if it survives. |

## Rejected, and why

| Candidate | Why not |
|---|---|
| SXSW | Right structure — roof brand over many verticals — wrong scale by three orders of magnitude. Its brand decisions are governed by constraints ODD will never have. Revisit only for the naming question. |
| Web Summit | Growth model is paid acquisition and regional franchising. Not available to ODD and not desirable. |
| Boiler Room | Genuinely instructive on content-first culture branding, but it is a media company that added events, the reverse of ODD's direction. Held in reserve. |
| Ars Electronica | Institutional, state-funded, 45 years old. Its marketing is a function of its institutional status, which is not transferable. |
| Any "creative agency Instagram" case study | No primary data available and no structural similarity. |
