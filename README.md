## The findings the strategy is built on

- **ODD has a capture problem, not an awareness problem.** 5,000+ people have been in
  an ODD room; **1,514 are reachable**, and 84% of those arrived in one August 2025
  import. Three to four thousand people came and cannot be contacted again.
- **Instagram is the biggest owned channel** — `@oddfest.co`, **4,409 followers** —
  and **the live website links to `@oddfest.fi`, which does not exist.**
- **The newsletter is the best asset and the most neglected** — **60.9% median open
  rate**, silent 80 days, growing at 4.5 people a month, 92% unattributed.
- **People beat pages by 2.5–3×**, confirmed independently on LinkedIn (533
  reactions), Instagram (3.1% vs 1.1%) and in the Buildspace teardown (2.5×).
- **Instagram carries the feeling; email carries the instruction.** ODD's best
  Instagram post is a thank-you; its worst is a feedback form. On email it inverts —
  utility clicks 9.46%, lineup drops click 2%.
- **Slush already built and killed the two sub-brands ODD is about to build.**
  *Soaked by Slush* and *Node by Slush* are both 404.
